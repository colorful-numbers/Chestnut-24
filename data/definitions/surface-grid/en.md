# Surface Grid

A distributed surface network formed after the Miracle: compact settlements, supply stations, and maintained routes occur at a stable density, letting solitary travelers cross the land safely without depending on large cities.

Each settlement compresses food, medicine, repair, communication, and temporary shelter into a small footprint. The land between nodes is left to recover, while resource coverage avoids gaps large enough to strand a traveler without support.
